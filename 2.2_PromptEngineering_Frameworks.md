# Overview of AI Prompts Framework

## Textual Analysis & Response Frameworks
- ### A.P.E. (Answer, Prove, Explain)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: A method to structure detailed responses. The AI must Answer the query, prove by citing data or sources, and explain the relevance or implications.
    **Application**: Useful in educational tech, customer support, and content generation.
    **Additional Resources**: This framework encourages depth and accountability in responses, promoting transparency in AI interactions.
    
<image src="./images/APE_Prompt.png" alt="APE_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

- ###  R.A.C.E. (Restate, Answer, Cite, Explain)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: This framework requires the AI to Restate the question, answer it directly, cite sources or data, and explain the context.
    **Application**: Ideal for journalism AI, academic research tools, and any AI required to support claims with evidence.
    **Additional Resources**: R.A.C.E enhances reliability and factual consistency, critical for applications involving information dissemination.

<image src="./images/RACE_Prompt.png" alt="RACE_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

- ### E.R.A. (Evidence, Reasoning, Assertion)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: Prompts the AI to base its communication on Evidence, strengthen it through Reasoning, and make an Assertion.
    **Application**: Utilized in debate AIs, legal analysis tools, and ethical decision-making systems.
    **Additional Resources**: By structuring thoughts clearly, E.R.A helps AIs articulate reasoned arguments and decisions.

<image src="./images/ERA_Prompt.png" alt="ERA_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

- ### TRACE(Text, Reader, Author, Context, Exigence)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: Analyzes textual information thoroughly through five dimensions: Text, Reader, Author, Context, and Exigence.
    **Application**: Suitable for literary analysis tools, in-depth content exploration, and academic research.
    **Additional Resources**: T.R.A.C.E provides a multi-dimensional approach to text, enhancing understanding and interpretation in AI.

<image src="./images/TRACE_Prompt.png" alt="TRACE_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

## Narrative & Reflective Structuring Frameworks

- ### C.O.A.S.T. (Context, Obstacle, Action, Solution, Transformation)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: Structures complex problem-solving tasks by defining Context, identifying Obstacles, suggesting Actions, offering Solutions, and describing potential Transformations.
    **Application**: Helpful in strategic planning tools, AI-driven consultancy, and management simulations.
    **Additional Resources**:This framework fosters a comprehensive approach to tackling challenges, making it suitable for AI systems in managerial contexts.
    
<image src="./images/COAST_Prompt.png" alt="COAST_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

- ###  R.I.S.E. (Reflect, Inquire, Suggest, Elevate) 
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: A feedback mechanism where the AI Reflects on the input, inquires for further clarification, suggests improvements, and elevates the discussion.
    **Application**: Effective in peer review systems, creative collaborative platforms, and coaching AIs.
    **Additional Resources**: R.I.S.E encourages a constructive feedback loop, integral in collaborative and educational AI applications.

<image src="./images/RISE_Prompt.png" alt="RISE_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

- ### R.O.S.E.S. (Recap, Observe, Strategize, Evaluate, Suggest)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: A methodical approach where the AI must Recap the situation, observe details, strategize solutions, evaluate outcomes, and Suggest improvements.
    **Application**: Useful in decision-making AI used in business intelligence, disaster response, and project management.
    **Additional Resources**: This comprehensive problem-solving sequence is ideal for complex scenarios requiring careful consideration and tactical planning.
    
<image src="./images/ROSE_Prompt.png" alt="ROSE_Prompt" style="width:550px"/><br>

## Literary Identification, Values-Based & Ethical Reasoning Frameworks

- ### T.A.G. (Title, Author, Genre)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: A simple framework for organizing and recalling literary information. The AI identifies the Title, Author, and Genre of a work.
    **Application**: Utilized in educational AI, library assistive technologies, and content curation platforms.
    **Additional Resources**: T.A.G helps in quick retrieval of essential information, enhancing user interaction in educational contexts.

<image src="./images/TAG_Prompt.png" alt="Menu_Actions" style="width:550px"/><br>

- ### C.A.R.E. (Consideration, Action, Responsibility, Empathy)
 Explaination: 
    **Description**: Encourages AIs to show Consideration, propose Actions, take Responsibility, and demonstrate Empathy.
    **Application**: Key in customer service robots, healthcare assistants, and therapeutic chatbots.
    **Additional Resources**: C.A.R.E. promotes humane and empathetic interactions, vital for AI in roles that require sensitivity.

<image src="./images/CARE_Prompt.png" alt="Menu_Actions" style="width:550px"/><br>
